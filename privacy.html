This page explains what happens to information when you use this website. Terms of Service.

Who we are

Bend Grub operates this website (https://bendgrub.com) in Bend, Oregon. The restaurant is delivery only. You can reach us on Instagram at @z_grub_burgerz.

What this website does

This Site shows the menu and how to order. You cannot create an account here, and you cannot pay for food on this Site.

Orders

Every online order is placed on DoorDash, at https://order.online/store/23259674. DoorDash collects the name, contact details, payment information, and delivery details for that order, under DoorDash's own terms and privacy policy. We receive what we need to prepare the order. Questions about a charge, a receipt, or a DoorDash account go to DoorDash.

A price printed on this Site can lag behind the app. The price, tax, and any fee shown at DoorDash checkout are the ones that apply.

Information you give us

If you message us on Instagram, we use what you send to answer you. This Site does not have an account, a mailing list, or a contact form.

Information collected automatically

The companies that host and deliver this Site (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) may keep ordinary connection logs: IP address, browser type, the date and time, and the page requested. Those logs are used to run and protect the Site. We do not install advertising cookies, and we do not run Google Analytics or another traffic tracker on this Site.

This Site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address when that file is requested.

Links that leave this Site

The DoorDash and Instagram links take you to someone else's site. Their privacy policy applies once you leave.

We do not sell personal information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context advertising.

Children

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Bend Grub, Bend, Oregon. Message us on Instagram at @z_grub_burgerz.
